Who We Are

Fragrance as Identity

ASEEL was founded on a simple idea: a fragrance should feel like an extension of the person wearing it — not a trend borrowed from somewhere else. Working from Bangladesh, we set out to build a perfume house that could stand beside the world's most respected fragrance names, without losing what makes it ours.

Every ASEEL fragrance begins with raw materials chosen for depth and honesty — real oud, real florals, real amber — and is refined over multiple trial batches until the balance feels right.
