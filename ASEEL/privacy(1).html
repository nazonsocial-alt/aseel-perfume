1. Introduction

ASEEL Perfume ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when you visit our website or place an order.

2. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect: your name, phone number, delivery address, and any order notes you provide. We do not collect payment card details on this website, as orders are confirmed directly via WhatsApp.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your order
  • To communicate with you about your order via WhatsApp, phone, or email
  • To respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form
  • To improve our products and website experience

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses your browser's local storage to remember items in your cart and wishlist. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Your order details are shared only with our internal team via WhatsApp to fulfill your order, and with delivery partners as needed to complete shipping.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at hello@aseelperfume.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
